# Privacy

Gimbo Galerie processes all photos, videos and settings exclusively on your device.

## Summary

- No internet permission, no ads, no analytics or tracking services
- No account, no sign-up, nothing is sent to the developer
- Edits, albums, favorites and projects are stored in private app storage
- App data is excluded from Android device backups

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Read photos and videos | Display, edit and manage your media |
| Media location | Show or keep the capture location only on request |
| Notifications | Progress of exports, backups and transfers |
| Foreground service, wake lock | Finish long tasks while the screen is off |
| Biometrics | Unlock the vault via the Android device lock |

## Vault

Vault content is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored in private app storage. The key is protected by the Android Keystore. The app neither reads nor stores biometric data or the device PIN. Uninstalling the app deletes the vault content.

## Optional cloud and folder backup

Backup is off by default. If you choose a location through Android (for example a cloud provider), files are copied there. The terms and privacy policy of the chosen provider apply to that data.

## Your rights

Since the developer receives no personal data, there is no stored data to disclose, correct or delete. You remove local data by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.
